**Verdict vocabulary** (used consistently below)

- **Supported** — the 4.0.0 record carries the field/value the requirement needs (consumer-side display conversion is allowed: contract §5.4 L161 says producers never convert edges to the app's display basis; BFF does).
- **Partial** — some needed data is present/derivable, but a required element is missing, ambiguous, or wrong in a case the requirement explicitly covers.
- **Not supported** — no 4.0.0 field/rule can satisfy it without a contract change (versioned addition or semantic change).
- **Out of scope for producer record** — presentation, interaction, or another service's data (schedule/live-scores/reference data); the record is not the right home and there is no contract defect, though the requirement still has a dependency.

**Method.** Every page requirement was read against all 37 fields (§6), the core producer rules (§3), lifecycle/status table (§4), edge/threshold math (§5), market/selection matrix (§7), JSON Schema (§9) and the five example records (§10). Where the page is silent, the embedded screenshots (attachments) were inspected; they are mocks and non-normative but expose fields the prose implies. The contract is a Draft: §11.1 open decisions (L883–891) and the pending sign-offs (L897) can change several verdicts — see §6.

### 2.4 Answers to the brief's special-attention questions

- **Recommended Action.** `rec_action` is reserved for exactly `"play"`, `"watch"`, `"pass"` but **must be null in 4.0.x** (§6.4 #34 L235; §9 L496). No derivation from `edge_status` is defined anywhere in the contract. A heuristic (`live`→play, `narrowing`→watch, `closed`→pass) is plausible but non-normative and breaks where `closed` means a line move, where `suspended` has no price, and where the page requires "current edge calculation" (which is null on a moved line). The secondary spec leaves thresholds to Product (D4, null until Nov 2). **Not supported; needs decision/field.**
- **Positive Edge odds threshold.** The contract fully specifies the math one consumer layer downstream: inputs on the record, b formulas per basis, floor-to-4-dp rounding, and P-4…P-8 vectors (§5.1 L136, L170–174). So it is *adequately specified as a computation* but *not carried as a value*, and the semantics are subtly different from "stays positive above X" unless `t=0`; it also cannot follow a line move. **Partial.**
- **Historical / pre-redesign picks.** Every 4.0.0 record has all 37 fields and `pick_date_time`/`entry_edge`/`probability` are never null (§2.1 L28; #13/#16/#19). A pre-redesign pick lacks edge/movement/timestamps, so it cannot be conformant, and no field marks it as historical or gives the cut-off. A consumer **cannot know** from a 4.0.0 record that a pick predates v4; nulls are indistinguishable from a new pick before its first observation. **Not supported — legacy documents/API version required.**
- **Prediction timestamp semantics.** `pick_date_time` is explicitly "when the pick was **first published**", frozen (§6.3 L212; Rule 3 L58); the page asks for "when it was **generated**". UTC ISO-8601-ms is supported (§2.4). No `generated_at`; the two times may differ. **Partial — clarify or add field (minor).**
- **Bookmaker name vs slug/display.** The record carries a lowercase slug (`entry_bookmaker_id` #24, e.g. `fanduel`), the source kind (`odds_source_kind` #23), and a separate current book (`current_bookmaker_id` #29). It does not carry the display name or casing; the US-book-name / AU-Avg-Odds presentation rule is consumer/region logic. **Partial — data present, display mapping consumer-side.**
- **Current line + updated edge on a moved line.** `current_line` is carried, but the contract **forces** `current_edge = null` and `edge_status = "closed"` whenever `current_line != line` (§5.1 L135; §5.3 priority 3 L153; field #32 L233; example §10.5), while the page explicitly requires the edge to be "recalculated based on the new line and odds" and the screenshots show a positive edge/Play after a move. There is **no probability-at-new-line field**, so the consumer cannot compute it either. **Not supported — direct conflict; needs a producer-supplied new-line edge or product descope (also D2).**
- **EPL Anytime Goalscorer / Asian Handicap (player/game filter supply).** EPL player props are "None in 4.0.0" (§7.3 L288) — Anytime Goalscorer is **Not supported** (could be an additive 4.x stat_code subject to §11.1). Asian Handicap has **no market token** (closed enum §6.2 L198/§9 L469; future tokens must be `[a-z0-9]+`, §8.2 L330); a whole/half AH is representable only as `spread`, and quarter-line AH (0.25/0.75 split stakes) is **explicitly not permitted** (§7.2 #5 L274). **Partial → Not supported** if a distinct/quarter-line AH is in scope. Also note baseline says EPL publishes no picks at all — product contradiction in §3.
- **Existing statuses vs contract vocabulary.** UI: Upcoming / Live / Final / Correct / Incorrect / Void. Contract: `open` / `awaiting_result` / `cancelled` / `settled`+`outcome won|lost|push` / `void` (+`withdrawn`). Mapping is possible but not defined in the contract; `Live`/`Final` are **game states**, and the game state is not carried by the record (needs schedule/live-scores join). `Correct/Incorrect/Void` are presentation of `outcome`. **Partial.**
- **Sorting and filters.** Buildable from `pick_kind` (#6), `sport` (#4), `entry_edge` (#19), `probability` (#13), `entry_decimal_odds` (#17)/`entry_american_odds` (#18); a current sort also has `current_edge` (#32)/`current_decimal_odds` (#25). "Edge" needs a decision: entry (frozen, always present) vs current (null on line move), and must be converted to one display basis before cross-sport comparison. Contract defines no sort/order rules (consumer concern). Filter *content* gaps are EPL Anytime Goalscorer and AH; Player/Game filter itself is fully supported.

### 4.3 Overall verdict (one paragraph)

Contract v4.0.0 is a good fit for the **core** of the redesign: identity/market selection, XO probability, projection, stake units, entry decimal+American odds and entry edge on a declared basis/threshold, the live current odds/line/observation timestamp/closing flag, cancellation with reason codes, and persistence/frozen-entry rules are all carried by the 37 fields and their invariants, so the ordinary card, probability wheel, Edge %, bookmaker slug, current odds, movement line, Edge-Availability *state* (via `edge_status`), cancelled cards and the All/Player/Game filter are implementable against it. Verdict: **the contract supports the majority of the redesign's data, but four blocking gaps must be closed by contract addition or product descope before the v6 design can be delivered from v4 records.**
